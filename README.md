# Formations Lab

Animated football formations for presenting to coaches. Each system shows its base shape, three build-up patterns, the final-third shape and the high, mid and low pressing blocks on an animated pitch, with each position's job, weak spots and training games. A coach view compares real average shapes and style numbers from StatsBomb open data, and an atlas shows formation usage across 7,922 starting line-ups.

- **Formations:** 4-3-3, 4-2-3-1, 4-4-2, 4-1-4-1, 3-5-2, 3-4-3, 3-4-2-1, 4-1-2-1-2. The 3-5-2, 4-1-4-1, 3-4-3 and 4-4-2 follow Álvaro Tato Estepa's SISTEMAS (@AlvaroTato2) and are shown against the same opponents as the document.
- **Coaches:** two coaches side by side or overlaid, in and out of possession, with possession, PPDA, press height and pass length.
- **Atlas:** formation shares by era and by men's and women's football, and the most and least flexible coaches.
- **Present:** full-screen mode. ← → phases, ↑ ↓ formations, space autoplay, esc exit.

## Stack

TanStack Start, React 19, Tailwind 4, Motion, Biome (Ultracite), Vitest, lefthook and commitlint, pnpm. Deployed on Vercel through Nitro.

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev        # http://localhost:3000
pnpm typecheck && pnpm lint && pnpm test
pnpm build
```

## Content and data

- `src/data/formations.ts` and `src/data/sistemas.ts`: positions per phase, arrows, roles, weak spots and drills. Coordinates run 0..100 from our goal to theirs and from our left touchline to our right.
- `src/lib/formations.ts`: turns that content into animation frames (blocks, opponent ghosts, arrows, ball).
- `src/data/statsbomb.json`: generated by `pnpm data` from [StatsBomb open data](https://github.com/statsbomb/open-data) (`scripts/data`). Please credit StatsBomb when presenting.
